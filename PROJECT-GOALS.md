# Project goals

This record defines a goal for each project in the portfolio. The active thread goal covers Project 1. The remaining entries are recorded goals that can be activated in turn. A recorded goal does not mean that the project is complete or that it is an active goal in the thread.

Each goal should be read with its original portfolio material, project plan and engineering pack. Those records contain the detailed scope and evidence requirements. The goals below do not replace them.

## Shared completion standard

For each project, implement the agreed scope with clear module boundaries and documented decisions. Use meaningful scenarios and tests that exercise successful operation, invalid input and relevant failures. Record the inputs, results and limits of each evaluation. Explain mathematical methods with defined symbols, units and checked examples where they are needed. Distinguish measured results from reference values and assumptions. Review the documentation and publish the project code and evidence to the Side Quests repository. Do not present synthetic results as real operational evidence.

## 01. Custom Autonomous Drone

**Status:** in progress. This is the active thread goal.

Complete the software reconstruction and integrate the simulation, estimation, control, mission planning, vision, power and telemetry work described in the project records. Test meaningful flight and fault scenarios and publish the verified software. Keep the account of the original aircraft separate from new work and record the limits of physical validation.

## 02. Aegis Autonomy

**Status:** planned.

Build and evaluate a simulation that compares fault monitors and degraded control under state estimation faults. Record the scenarios, monitor outcomes and evidence for each comparison. Treat the results as simulation evidence rather than physical safety or certification.

## 03. Infra Resilience

**Status:** planned.

Build and evaluate a synthetic material handling plant that records events and tests whether a diagnosis can distinguish an initiating fault from downstream alarms. Explain the ranked diagnoses and the limits of the evidence. Do not represent it as a certified protection function.

## 04. Proof AI

**Status:** planned.

Build and evaluate a document extraction workflow that compares independent checks and abstention against inappropriate automatic acceptance. Retain the supporting evidence and report the results on synthetic or openly licensed material. Do not claim certified compliance or realised savings.

## 05. Invariant Ops

**Status:** planned.

Build and test a procurement workflow that preserves its documented rules when messages are duplicated, delayed or interrupted. Provide reconciliation, correction and an audit record for synthetic orders, receipts and invoices. Do not claim a production ledger or exactly once transport.

## 06. Exchange Lab

**Status:** planned.

Build a deterministic matching engine with documented order rules and a reference implementation. Compare any optimisations against the reference using replay and differential tests. Benchmark only defined synthetic workloads and do not claim production exchange readiness.

## 07. Alpha Validity

**Status:** planned.

Build a reproducible research environment that tests how validation methods can mistake chance for skill in synthetic markets. Document the generators, candidate strategies, costs and statistical methods. Do not present synthetic results as tradable alpha or investment advice.

## 08. Freight Invoice Audit

**Status:** planned.

Build a rating and reconciliation workflow that reconstructs synthetic carrier charges from versioned terms and shipment events. Explain discrepancies with traceable supporting facts and route ambiguity for review. Do not claim actual recovery or submit disputes.

## 09. Delivery Matched Invoicing

**Status:** planned.

Build a workflow that reconciles synthetic purchase orders, deliveries, accepted quantities and supplier invoices. Record matching decisions and exceptions for human review. Do not execute payments or supplier disputes.

## 10. Maintenance Triage

**Status:** planned.

Build and evaluate a prototype that routes synthetic maintenance reports and escalates safety reports to a person. Document the evaluation and limits of automated decisions. Do not use it as a live safety system or dispatch service.

## 11. Company Hub

**Status:** planned.

Build a source based briefing prototype using synthetic organisations and local data. Include access filtering, traceable source records and reviewable entity links. Do not imply access to real organisational data or authorise outbound actions.

## 12. Workflow Evaluation Harness

**Status:** initial implementation in progress.

Complete the documented evaluation harness so a person can compare workflow candidates on versioned cases, inspect failures and review repeatable reports and gate outcomes. Test deliberately defective candidates and incomplete runs. A passing gate informs a human decision and does not authorise a release.
