How these results are calculated

Tracking error is simulated altitude minus target altitude. Estimation error is estimated altitude minus simulated altitude. RMS is the square root of the average squared error over all recorded samples. GPS is sampled at the configured interval, so its missing count only includes scheduled samples. Accepted and rejected counts refer to the filter innovation check.
