# Evidence and limitations

## Account of the earlier build

Lewis recalls a five inch racing frame, a four cell lithium polymer battery, a custom power distribution board, a Raspberry Pi 3 and an Arduino S3 mini. He recalls pressure and motion sensors, WiFi, Bluetooth and PID control. He also describes autonomous adjustment, computer vision, path planning, geolocation and a telemetry screen. The details of their implementation and measured performance are not available. This is an account from the builder. It is not a recovered schematic, firmware image or flight log.

## Material not available

The original source, schematics, board files, calibration records, build photos and flight logs have not been provided in the current workspace. The original portfolio PDFs are descriptions of the project rather than primary test logs. The exact hardware models, firmware versions and measured flight behaviour remain unverified.

## Consequence

The new code is a reconstruction and an illustrative test fixture. It cannot establish what the original aircraft did. It must not be connected to motors or used for flight. Any physical build needs separate electrical, flight safety and regulatory review. Claims about historical flight remain unsupported until evidence is available.
